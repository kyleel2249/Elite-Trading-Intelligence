Broker wallet: funding & fees

The broker provides the capital; ETI trades with it from the ETI wallet and pays the agreed fee back to the broker's wallet. ETI never holds the broker's key and cannot pull funds from it. Funding is counted only after the on-chain transfer is verified.

Parties

How funding works

  1. Create the ETI wallet on the ETI Wallet page.
  2. From the broker wallet, send native coin to the ETI address shown here.
  3. Paste the transaction hash below. ETI checks sender, recipient, success and confirmations on-chain, then counts it as allocated capital.
  4. ETI trades from that balance under the spending policy. Fees accrue on confirmed trades and are settled to the broker wallet with a previewed, confirmed payment.
