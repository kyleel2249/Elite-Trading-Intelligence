Use zoom controls to inspect price action; hold Shift and scroll over the chart to move through candle history. Candles are plotted from exchange data; nothing is simulated. If data cannot be loaded or is stale the chart says so instead of showing old values as live. Stock and forex candles are older than expected outside market hours and will be labelled stale. OANDA, Alpaca and Twelve Data need their credentials configured on the server; without them the market says so. Non-US stocks are listed as TICKER:EXCHANGE (for example SAP:XETR).
